# Unfollowers-Insta-version-vaporwave-aesthetic-
Browser-console tool to scan your Instagram following and find who doesn't follow you back. Retro-themed, Spanish UI.

Unfollowers is a lightweight tool that scans your Instagram following list and shows you who doesn't follow you back. It runs entirely in your browser's developer console — no install, no login to third parties, no data leaves your machine. Features a whitelist to protect accounts you want to keep, filters (non-followers, verified, private), and a paginated results view. Built on Preact, styled with a retro vaporwave theme.
